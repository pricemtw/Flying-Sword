⚔ Flying Sword

You are the sword. Speed is damage. Two blades, switch any time with Tab.

Straight swordAD turn · W boost · QE roll blade · S 180° spin (cuts + reverses)
TulwarWASD aim · J tap to slash, hold to charge. Each slash flings the blade that way. Slash into ground or uncuttable things to pogo.
StuckMash Space ×3. Hold WASD to choose which way you wrench out.
OtherM mouse mode (straight sword) · R reset

Straight sword: wide blade = edge-on, and it glints when sharp-aligned. Red = flat, which bounces. Turning twists the blade. Dive for stone.

Tulwar: only cuts while slashing, with power = charge × speed. The point and false edge (gold back edge) can bite and stick.
